Concept 03 - Archive Of Proof

Make long case studies feel curated, numbered, and evidential.

This direction is for the case-study system. It treats each project like an organized record rather than a blog post. It is especially strong for enterprise and architecture-heavy work where decisions, constraints, and outcomes matter as much as visuals.

Best for elevating the case-study pages and separating them from generic portfolio templates.
Works well with product, platform, and AI projects because it favors structure and evidence.

Concept 04 - Motion And Rollout

Choose one motion language, then apply it everywhere.

The update should not mix unrelated animation styles. Pick a signature language: smooth rail travel, soft showreel fades, dossier reveals, and tactile card states. This gives the portfolio a recognizable feel without over-designing every interaction.

This section is intended to become reusable motion notes inside the final Figma file.
Keep the system focused on reveal, emphasis, and hierarchy. Avoid playful motion unless it supports the story.

Rail transitions

Move between major scenes with a controlled camera path and a locked caption zone. The environment changes; the message stays steady.

Dossier reveal

Case-study modules can rise in with opacity plus slight vertical travel. Keep the motion short and documentary in tone.

Showreel hover

Featured cards should preview a caption change, image shift, or subtle light sweep rather than a generic scale effect.

Movie Vault as lab

Give the experimental work a slightly different behavior set: richer contrast, stronger motion, and more obvious craft cues.
